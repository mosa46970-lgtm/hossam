A selection of BIM and digital engineering works developed using **Civil 3D, Navisworks, Subassembly Composer, Dynamo, Inventor, IPE, Part Builder, and Catalog Part Editor**. The work includes **3D modeling, BIM coordination, custom assemblies and components, design automation, shop drawings, model review, and clash detection**, with a focus on **accuracy, coordination, efficiency, and developing smart engineering workflows**.
